::: info 题目

求积分 $\ds\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}}{\left( \frac{\sin nx}{\sin x} \right) ^2\text{d}x}$ .

:::

<!-- more -->

题目已经明显暗示了，构造差分即可.

$$
\begin{aligned}
    I_{n+1}-I_n&=\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}}{\frac{\sin ^2\left( n+1 \right) x-\sin ^2nx}{\sin ^2x}\text{d}x}\\
    &=\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}}{\frac{\sin \left( 2n+1 \right) x\sin x}{\sin ^2x}\text{d}x}\\
    &=\frac{1}{2}\int_0^{\pi}{\frac{\sin \left( 2n+1 \right) x}{\sin x}\text{d}x}\\
\end{aligned}
$$

可得

$$
\int_0^{\pi}{\frac{\sin (2n+1)x}{\sin x}\mathrm d x}=\pi
$$

故

$$
I_n=I_1+\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}{\left( I_{k+1}-I_k \right)}=\frac{\pi}{2}+\frac{\left( n-1 \right) \pi}{2}=\frac{n\pi}{2}
$$
