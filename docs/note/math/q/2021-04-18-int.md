::: info 题目

计算积分 $\ds\int_0^{+\infty}{\text{e}^{-x}|\cos x|\text{d}x}$ .

:::

<!-- more -->


> <https://www.zhihu.com/question/455190275>

提供两种思路

首先注意如下事实：
$$
\int{\text{e}^{ax}\cos bx\text{d}x}=\frac{1}{a^2+b^2}\left| \begin{matrix}
	\left( \text{e}^{ax} \right) '&		\left( \cos bx \right) '\\
	\text{e}^{ax}&		\cos bx\\
\end{matrix} \right|=\frac{\text{e}^{ax}\left( a\cos bx+b\sin bx \right)}{a^2+b^2}
$$
法1：
$$
\begin{aligned}
    I&=\int_0^{+\infty}{\text{e}^{-x}|\cos x|\text{d}x}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}{\int_{n\pi}^{\left( n+1 \right) \pi}{\text{e}^{-x}|\cos x|\text{d}x}}\\
    &=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}{\int_0^{\pi}{\text{e}^{-\left( x+n\pi \right)}|\cos x|\text{d}x}}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}{\text{e}^{-n\pi}\int_0^{\pi}{\text{e}^{-x}\cos x\text{d}x}}\\
    &=\left[ \frac{\text{e}^{-x}\left( -\cos x+\sin x \right)}{2} \right] _{0}^{\pi}\times \sum_{n=0}^{\infty}{\left( \text{e}^{-\pi} \right) ^n}\\
    &=\frac{1+\text{e}^{-\pi}}{2}\frac{1}{1-\text{e}^{-\pi}}=\frac{1}{2}\frac{\text{e}^{\pi}+1}{\text{e}^{\pi}-1}\\
\end{aligned}
$$


法2：
$$
\begin{aligned}
	I&=\int_0^{+\infty}{\text{e}^{-x}|\cos x|\text{d}x}=\int_0^{\pi}{+\int_{\pi}^{+\infty}{\text{e}^{-x}|\cos x|\text{d}x}}\\
	&=\int_0^{\pi}{\text{e}^{-x}\cos x\text{d}x}+\overset{x\mapsto x+\pi}{\overbrace{\int_{\pi}^{+\infty}{\text{e}^{-x}|\cos x|\text{d}x}}}\\
	&=\frac{1+\text{e}^{-\pi}}{2}+\text{e}^{-\pi}I\\
	\Rightarrow I&=\frac{1+\text{e}^{-\pi}}{2\left( 1-\text{e}^{-\pi} \right)}=\frac{1}{2}\frac{\text{e}^{\pi}+1}{\text{e}^{\pi}-1}\\
\end{aligned}
$$
