::: info 题目

计算积分 $\ds\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}}{\frac{\sqrt{\sin ^3x\cos x}}{1+\sin x\cos x}\text{d}x}$ .

:::

<!-- more -->

> 此题解法源自笔者在 知乎[^1] 的回答。

## 解法

### 常规思路

利用区间再现，凑微分即可：

$$
\begin{aligned}
	I&\xlongequal{x\mapsto \frac{\pi}{2}-x}\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}}{\frac{\cos x\sqrt{\sin x\cos x}}{1+\sin x\cos x}\text{d}x}=\frac{1}{2}\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}}{\frac{\left( \sin x+\cos x \right) \sqrt{-\frac{1}{2}\left( \sin x-\cos x \right) ^2+\frac{1}{2}}}{\frac{3}{2}-\frac{1}{2}\left( \sin x-\cos x \right) ^2}\text{d}x}\\
	&=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}}{\frac{\sqrt{1-\left( \sin x-\cos x \right) ^2}}{3-\left( \sin x-\cos x \right) ^2}\text{d}\left( \sin x-\cos x \right)}=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\int_{-1}^1{\frac{\sqrt{1-t^2}}{3-t^2}\text{d}t}\\
	&=\sqrt{2}\int_0^1{\frac{\sqrt{1-t^2}}{3-t^2}\text{d}t}\xlongequal{t=\sin u}\sqrt{2}\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}}{\frac{\cos ^2u}{3-\sin ^2u}\text{d}u}=\sqrt{2}\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}}{\left( 1-\frac{2}{3-\sin ^2u} \right) \text{d}u}\\
	&=\frac{\pi}{\sqrt{2}}-2\sqrt{2}\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}}{\frac{\text{d}\left( \tan u \right)}{3\left( 1+\tan ^2u \right) -\tan ^2u}}=\frac{\pi}{\sqrt{2}}-2\sqrt{2}\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}}{\frac{\,\text{d}\left( \tan u \right)}{3+2\tan ^2u}}\\
	&=\frac{\pi}{\sqrt{2}}-\frac{2\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{2}\sqrt{3}}\left[ \arctan \frac{\sqrt{2}\tan u}{\sqrt{3}} \right] _{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}=\frac{\pi}{\sqrt{2}}-\frac{\pi}{\sqrt{3}}\\
\end{aligned}
$$

### 技巧

利用 Cauchy-Schlömilch 变换[^2]：

$$
\begin{aligned}
	I&=\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}}{\frac{\tan x\sqrt{\tan x}}{\sec ^2x+\tan x}\text{d}x}\xlongequal{t=\sqrt{\tan x}}\int_0^{\infty}{\frac{t^3}{1+t^4+t^2}\cdot \frac{2t}{1+t^4}\text{d}t}\\
	&=\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}{\frac{1}{\frac{1}{t^2}+t^2+1}\frac{1}{\frac{1}{t^2}+t^2}\text{d}t}=\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}{\frac{1}{\left( t-\frac{1}{t} \right) ^2+3}\frac{1}{\left( t-\frac{1}{t} \right) ^2+2}\text{d}t}\\
	&\xlongequal{\text{Cauchy-Schl}\ddot{\text{o}}\text{milch}}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}{\frac{1}{t^2+3}\frac{1}{t^2+2}\text{d}t}=2\int_0^{+\infty}{\left( \frac{1}{t^2+2}-\frac{1}{t^2+3} \right) \text{d}t}\\
	&=\frac{\pi}{\sqrt{2}}-\frac{\pi}{\sqrt{3}}\\
\end{aligned}
$$
