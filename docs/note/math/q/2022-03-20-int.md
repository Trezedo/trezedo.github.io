::: info 题目

计算 $\ds\int_0^{\pi}{\frac{\text{d}x}{1+\sin ^4x}}$ 和 $\ds\int_0^{\pi}{\frac{\sin }{1+\sin ^4x}\text{d}x}$ .

:::

<!-- more -->

Cauchy-Schlömilch 变换我们曾经在 [2020-09-10积分题](2020-09-10-int.md) 中使用过。

这里给出能使用它的两道积分题。
$$
\begin{aligned}
	I&=\int_0^{\pi}{\frac{\text{d}x}{1+\sin ^4x}}=2\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}}{\frac{1+\tan ^2x}{\left( 1+\tan ^2x \right) ^2+\tan ^4x}\text{d}\left( \tan x \right)}\xlongequal{t=\tan x}2\int_0^{+\infty}{\frac{1+t^2}{2t^4+2t^2+1}\text{d}t}\\
	&=2\int_0^{+\infty}{\frac{\frac{1}{t^2}+1}{2t^2+2+\frac{1}{t^2}}\text{d}t}=2\int_0^{+\infty}{\frac{\text{d}\left( \sqrt{2}t-\frac{1}{t} \right) +\left( 1-\sqrt{2} \right) \text{d}t}{\left( \sqrt{2}t-\frac{1}{t} \right) ^2+2+2\sqrt{2}}}\\
	&=\xlongequal{u=\sqrt{2}t-t^{-1}}2\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}{\frac{\text{d}u}{u^2+\left( 2+2\sqrt{2} \right)}}+\frac{1-\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{2}}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}{\frac{\text{d}t}{t^2+\left( 2+2\sqrt{2} \right)}}\\
	&=\left( 2+\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}-1 \right) \cdot 2\left[ \frac{1}{\sqrt{2+2\sqrt{2}}}\arctan \frac{u}{\sqrt{2+2\sqrt{2}}} \right] _{0}^{\infty}\\
	&=\frac{\sqrt{2}+1}{\sqrt{2}}\cdot \frac{\pi}{\sqrt{2}\sqrt{1+\sqrt{2}}}=\frac{\pi}{2}\sqrt{1+\sqrt{2}}\\
\end{aligned}
$$



$$
\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}{\frac{\text{d}\left( \sqrt{2}t-\frac{1}{t} \right)}{\left( \sqrt{2}t-\frac{1}{t} \right) ^2+2+2\sqrt{2}}}\xlongequal{u=\sqrt{2}t-t^{-1}}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}{\frac{\text{d}u}{u^2+\left( 2+2\sqrt{2} \right)}}
$$
