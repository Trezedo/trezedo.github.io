::: info 题目

计算 $\ds\int_0^{\pi}{\frac{\text{d}x}{1+\sin ^4x}}$ 和 $\ds\int_0^{\pi}{\frac{\sin x}{1+\sin ^4x}\text{d}x}$ .

:::

<!-- more -->

Cauchy-Schlömilch 变换我们曾经在 [2020-09-10 积分题](2020-09-10-int.md) 中使用过。

这里给出能使用它的两道积分题。

$$
\begin{aligned}
    I&=\int_0^{\pi}{\frac{{\rm d}x}{1+\sin ^4x}}=2\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}}{\frac{1+\tan ^2x}{(1+\tan ^2x)^2+\tan ^4x}{\rm d}(\tan x)}=2\int_0^{+\infty}{\frac{1+t^2}{2t^4+2t^2+1}{\rm d}t}\\
    &=2\int_0^{+\infty}{\frac{\frac{1}{t^2}+1}{2t^2+2+\frac{1}{t^2}}{\rm d}t}=2\int_0^{+\infty}{\frac{{\rm d}\left( \sqrt{2}t-\frac{1}{t} \right) +\left( 1-\sqrt{2} \right) {\rm d}t}{\left( \sqrt{2}t-\frac{1}{t} \right) ^2+2+2\sqrt{2}}}\\
    &\xlongequal{u=\sqrt{2}t-t^{-1}}2\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}{\frac{{\rm d}u}{u^2+\left( 2+2\sqrt{2} \right)}}+\frac{1-\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{2}}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}{\frac{{\rm d}t}{t^2+\left( 2+2\sqrt{2} \right)}}\\
    &=\left( 2+\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}-1 \right) \cdot 2\left[ \frac{1}{\sqrt{2+2\sqrt{2}}}\arctan \frac{u}{\sqrt{2+2\sqrt{2}}} \right] _{0}^{\infty}\\
    &=\frac{\sqrt{2}+1}{\sqrt{2}}\cdot \frac{\pi}{\sqrt{2}\sqrt{1+\sqrt{2}}}=\frac{\pi}{2}\sqrt{1+\sqrt{2}}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}{\frac{\text{d}\left( \sqrt{2}t-\frac{1}{t} \right)}{\left( \sqrt{2}t-\frac{1}{t} \right) ^2+2+2\sqrt{2}}}\xlongequal{u=\sqrt{2}t-t^{-1}}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}{\frac{\text{d}u}{u^2+\left( 2+2\sqrt{2} \right)}}
$$
