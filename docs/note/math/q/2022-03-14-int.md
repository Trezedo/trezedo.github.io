::: info 题目

计算不定积分 $\ds I=\int{\frac{\text{d}x}{\left( x^2+a^2 \right) ^2}}$.

:::

<!-- more -->

## 常规思路

### 凑微分

直接凑微分得到
$$
I=\int{-\frac{1}{2x}\text{d}\left( \frac{1}{x^2+a^2} \right)}=-\frac{1}{2x}\frac{1}{x^2+a^2}+\frac{1}{2}\int{\frac{1}{x^2+a^2}\text{d}\left( \frac{1}{x} \right)}
$$
其中
$$
\begin{aligned}
	\int{\frac{1}{x^2+a^2}\text{d}\left( \frac{1}{x} \right)}&=\frac{1}{a^3}\int{\frac{\frac{a^2}{x^2}}{1+\frac{a^2}{x^2}}\text{d}\left( \frac{a}{x} \right)}=\frac{1}{a^3}\int{1-\frac{1}{1+\frac{a^2}{x^2}}\text{d}\left( \frac{a}{x} \right)}\\
	&=\frac{1}{a^2}\frac{1}{x}-\frac{1}{a^3}\arctan \frac{a}{x}\\
	&=\frac{1}{a^2}\frac{1}{x}+\frac{1}{a^3}\arctan \frac{x}{a}-\frac{\pi}{2a^3}\\
\end{aligned}
$$
因此
$$
\begin{aligned}
	I&=-\frac{1}{2x\left( x^2+a^2 \right)}+\frac{1}{2a^2x}+\frac{1}{2a^3}\arctan \frac{x}{a}\\
	&=\frac{1}{2a^2x}\left( 1-\frac{a^2}{x^2+a^2} \right) +\frac{1}{2a^3}\arctan \frac{x}{a}\\
	&=\frac{1}{2a^2}\frac{x}{x^2+a^2}+\frac{1}{2a^3}\arctan \frac{x}{a}+C\\
\end{aligned}
$$

### 三角换元

看到 $x^2+a^2$ 很容易想到三角换元：
$$
\begin{aligned}
	\int{\frac{\text{d}x}{\left( x^2+a^2 \right) ^2}}&\xlongequal{x=a\tan u}\int{\frac{a\sec ^2u\text{d}u}{a^4\left( \tan ^2u+1 \right) ^2}}=\frac{1}{a^3}\int{\frac{\text{d}u}{\sec ^2u}}=\frac{1}{a^3}\int{\cos ^2u\text{d}u}\\
	&=\frac{1}{a^3}\int{\frac{1+\cos 2u}{2}\text{d}u}=\frac{u}{2a^3}+\frac{1}{4a^3}\sin 2u\\
	&=\frac{1}{2a^3}\arctan \frac{x}{a}+\frac{1}{4a^3}\frac{2\frac{x}{a}}{1+\left( \frac{x}{a} \right) ^2}\\
	&=\frac{1}{2a}\arctan \frac{x}{a}+\frac{1}{2a^2}\frac{x}{a^2+x^2}+C\\
\end{aligned}
$$
其中：
$$
\begin{aligned}
	\sin \left( 2\arctan x \right) &=2\sin \left( \arctan x \right) \cos \left( \arctan x \right)\\
	&=2\cdot \frac{x}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}\frac{1}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}\\
	&=\frac{2x}{1+x^2}\\
\end{aligned}
$$

## 其他思路

注意到如下递推式：
$$
I_{n+1}=\frac{1}{2a^2n}\left[ \frac{x}{\left( x^2+a^2 \right) ^n}+\left( 2n-1 \right) I_n \right]
$$
其中 $\ds I_n=\int{\frac{\text{d}x}{\left( x^2+a^2 \right) ^n}}$ ，且 $n\geqslant 1$ ，那么
$$
\begin{aligned}
	I&=I_2=\frac{1}{2a^2}\left[ \frac{x}{x^2+a^2}+I_1 \right] =\frac{1}{2a^2}\left[ \frac{x}{x^2+a^2}+\int{\frac{\text{d}x}{x^2+a^2}} \right]\\
	&=\frac{1}{2a^2}\left[ \frac{x}{x^2+a^2}+\frac{1}{a}\arctan \frac{x}{a} \right] +C\\
\end{aligned}
$$

::: tip 证明

直接分部积分
$$
\begin{aligned}
	I_n&=\int{\frac{\text{d}x}{\left( x^2+a^2 \right) ^n}}=\frac{x}{\left( x^2+a^2 \right) ^n}-\int{x\text{d}\left[ \frac{1}{\left( x^2+a^2 \right) ^n} \right]}\\
	&=\frac{x}{\left( x^2+a^2 \right) ^n}-\int{x\frac{-n\cdot 2x}{\left( x^2+a^2 \right) ^{n+1}}\text{d}x}\\
	&=\frac{x}{\left( x^2+a^2 \right) ^n}+2n\int{\frac{x^2+a^2-a^2}{\left( x^2+a^2 \right) ^{n+1}}\text{d}x}\\
	&=\frac{x}{\left( x^2+a^2 \right) ^n}+2n\left( I_n-a^2I_{n+1} \right)\\
\end{aligned}
$$
化简即得：
$$
I_{n+1}=\frac{1}{2a^2n}\left[ \frac{x}{\left( x^2+a^2 \right) ^n}+\left( 2n-1 \right) I_n \right]
$$
:::

实际上这也相当于三角函数 $n$ 次幂积分的递推式。
